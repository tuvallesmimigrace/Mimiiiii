About Mims Web

A small studio that treats your business like our own

Mims Web is a web development and design team based in Bayawan City, Negros Oriental. We help local shops, schools and growing businesses get online with websites that look good, load fast and are easy to manage.

  • One team for design, development and marketing
  • Clear pricing and timelines before we start
  • You own your website, domain and content
  • Friendly support in English, Filipino and Bisaya
